3 Things to Clean Your Home Air Today

Powered by Gemini AI • Kolkata Urban Hacks
1️⃣ Wet Mopping Instead of Dry Sweeping

In Kolkata homes, dry broom sweeping (ঝাড়ু) flings microscopic PM2.5, road silt, and soot back into breathing height where it hangs for 4+ hours. Use a damp microfiber mop with water and a pinch of salt or neem liquid. It traps and flushes particulates down the drain permanently.

2️⃣ Smart Ventilation Window (12:30 PM - 3:30 PM)

Never open windows during early morning (6 AM - 10 AM) or evening rush hours (6 PM - 9 PM) when bus and auto exhausts are trapped by cold air. Open balcony and bedroom windows strictly between 12:30 PM and 3:30 PM when afternoon solar warmth lifts the ground inversion layer.

3️⃣ Low-Cost Kolkata Purifying Plants

Place 2-3 local low-maintenance indoor plants like Areca Palm (লিভিং রুম পাম), Snake Plant (সাপ গাছ), and Money Plant in living areas. Snake plants release oxygen at night, absorbing common urban volatile compounds (benzene and formaldehyde) with zero electricity bill.
